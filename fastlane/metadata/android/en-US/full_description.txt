A uniquely satisfying puzzle challenge where an n x n square grid is broken into pieces, and it’s up to you to put them back together! Simple to learn yet deeply engaging, it’s the ultimate brain teaser for puzzle lovers of all ages.

HOW TO PLAY:

- Analyze the broken pieces of the n x n square.
- Drag, drop, and rotate shapes to fit them seamlessly back into place.
- Clear increasingly complex levels as the grid scales up!
